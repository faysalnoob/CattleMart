Validation limits
The automated suite covers database setup, image decoding, selected item details, order persistence, duplicate checkout, ownership filters, list construction and confirmation control placement. It does not exhaustively click every UI control or test a real payment provider. Production deployment and arbitrary legacy database migrations are outside this validation.
